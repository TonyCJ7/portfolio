## `src/app/workshop/layout.tsx`

### [WorkshopLayout({ children })](../../../../../src/app/workshop/layout.tsx#L9)

Wraps every Workshop page in the Workshop header: the `LogoLink` mark linking home and the word "Workshop" in mono label style.

- In: `children`, the entry page.
- Out: a full-height column with the header above the page.

## Constants

- [`metadata`](../../../../../src/app/workshop/layout.tsx#L4): the Workshop title and description; an entry page overrides the title with its own name.
