## `src/sections/Contact/Contact.workshop.tsx`

## Constants

- [`entry`](../../../../../../src/sections/Contact/Contact.workshop.tsx#L5): the Contact Workshop entry in the Sections group, with no controls. On desktop it renders the section as it is. On a phone it renders the section in a 480 px tall scroll area, the same scene as the other sections. The section is short enough there that nothing scrolls and the label bar stays at the top of the area.
